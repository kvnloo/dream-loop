## Detail that remains domain-specific

Keep camera-ray visibility filtering, direct notch/extrude stair edits, measured face flushness, UV Jacobians, projection registration, optical meshes, native hair/ivy APIs and exact material transplants in Blender domain skills. Their old project-specific values are examples, not universal constants. Preserve conflicting failed methods (warped tops, fresh fitted stair boxes, broad reflection sheets, hair-only moss) as negative evidence.

The nine lookdev recipes cover HDR strokes, glass eye, wet stone/dew, bioluminescence, clear coat, painterly surface, moss/ivy, bloom/exposure and projection. The cookbook reports rendered controls and a rejected initial botanical coupon; mechanism success never established final owner acceptance. Its artifact links must be relocated or preserved when installing a portable domain pack.

The orb's decisive fixed-view interior used target-assisted projection on curved layers; that is a declared hero-view representation, not recovered arbitrary-view volumetric structure. Later integrated previews were independently preferred while the brief remained incomplete. Do not turn “KEEP AS WORKING PREVIEW” into “finished.”

## Consolidation of overlapping critics

Absorb the reusable methods from `tiled-dreamloop-readonly`, `tiled-dreamloop-critic`, and `tiled-readonly-dreamloop-critic` into [critics](../references/critics.md), scripts and templates. Their unique contributions retained here are source byte-locks, core/padded coverage, exact contracts, native session/image-load receipts, delivered-resolution checks, blocked-null scoring, deduplicated issue evidence, disagreement adjudication and raw-versus-owner tier separation.

Reduce `quackles-scene-dreamloop` to a project adapter/case reference: current owner decisions, canonical artifact roots, actual fixture/locks, named recipes and viewer environment. Do not delete its Blender craft; move general craft into `blender-reference-match-loop` and local constants into its case references first. Deletion/install decisions belong to the parent/owner; this pack does not remove profile skills.
