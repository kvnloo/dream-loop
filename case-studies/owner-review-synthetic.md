# Synthetic owner-review case study

This is a fictional example, not a record of any owner's project, conversation,
render, preference, trial count or acceptance decision. Project-specific trial
inventories and chat-ID verdict maps are deliberately omitted from the public
seed helper and documentation.

A demo project has a panel with two candidate treatments, `sample-A` and
`sample-B`. Its local event ledger records measured artifacts and unknown costs.
An external catalog manifest selects the images and comparison. A synthetic
component receipt says “Synthetic feedback: retain the panel edge.” It is not
whole-trial acceptance and supplies no star rating.

The owner-review UI can confirm that scoped receipt or record a separate trial
rating. The payoff learner counts only the relevant scoped evidence. A recipe
remains unqualified until all required native-density, artifact-integrity and
acceptance gates are satisfied. Prefix-only replay leaves unsupported outcomes
and policy values null; this example establishes no scheduler advantage.

Use `scripts/synthetic_fixture.py` to generate colored pixels and synthetic
receipts in a new disposable directory. Never replace those fixtures with real
chats or renders when preparing a public change. Private deployments supply
manifests and receipts locally; those inputs are not distributable source.
